---

### How to use these records

- One record per feature and per cross-cutting decision. Numbering is sequential; never reuse a number.
- Before changing a feature, read its record(s). If the change contradicts a decision, write a new record that **supersedes** the old one and mark the old one `Superseded by NNNN`. Do not edit the old decision text.
- Small clarifications that do not change a decision may be appended under a dated "Notes" heading.
